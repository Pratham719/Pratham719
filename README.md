## ⭐ Featured Projects

**📊 Nassau Candy — Profitability & Margin Analysis**
3 divisions analyzed across 2 years of sales data.
Chocolate alone drove 95%+ of total profit — Sugar and Other divisions
flagged as margin risk despite active revenue.
Top 5 products accounted for ~80% of gross profit.
→ [Live Dashboard](https://margin-performance-dashboard.streamlit.app) · [Repo](https://github.com/Pratham719/Nassau-Candy-Analysis-v2)

**☕ Afficionado Coffee — Sales & Product Mix Analysis**
9 product categories · 80 products analyzed across store locations and product types.
Top 42 products (52.5%) drive 80% of total revenue — remaining 38 contribute only 20%.
Moderate concentration risk flagged: single-product dominance within Tea and 
Drinking Chocolate divisions signals menu dependency worth monitoring.
→ [Live Dashboard](https://coffee-revenue-dashboard-2026.streamlit.app) · [Repo](https://github.com/Pratham719/Afficionado-coffee-roster)
